# --description--

Katika maabara hii, utashughulikia maagizo ya biashara mtandao na kushughulikia makosa yanayotokea njiani.

**Lengo:** Timiza hadithi za watumizi zilizo hapa chini na upite vipimo vyote ili kumaliza maabara.

Data utakayofanya nayo kazi ina muundo sawa na vigezo katika msimbo wa kuanzia:

- **Agizo** ni kamusi yenye:
  - `order_id`: nambari kamili inayotambulisha agizo.
  - `products`: orodha ya kamusi, kila moja ikiwa na `product_id` na `quantity` iliyohitajika.
  - `discount_code`: mfuatano wa herufi, au `None` ikiwa agizo halina msimbo wa punguzo.
  - `status`: mfuatano wa herufi unaoelezea hali ya agizo.
- **Historia ya maagizo** ni orodha ya maagizo.
- **Hifadhidata ya bidhaa** ni kamusi ambayo funguo zake ni nambari za bidhaa na thamani zake ni kamusi zenye jina la `product`, `quantity` iliyopo kwenye hisa, na `price` ya kitengo kimoja.
- **Misimbo ya punguzo** ni kamusi ambayo funguo zake ni misimbo na thamani zake ni viwango vya punguzo, kwa mfano `0.1` kwa punguzo la asilimia 10.

**Hadithi za watumizi:**

1. Unapaswa kuwa na kitendakazi kinachoitwa `find_order` chenye vigezo viwili: nambari ya agizo na rekodi ya maagizo, kwa mpangilio huo.
1. `find_order` inapaswa:
   - rudisha agizo kutoka rekodi ya maagizo lililotolewa lenye `order_id` sawa na nambari ya agizo iliyotolewa.
   - toa `ValueError` yenye ujumbe `Order ID {order_id} not found in order history.` ikiwa hakuna agizo linalolingana na nambari ya agizo iliyotolewa.
1. Unapaswa kuwa na kitendakazi kinachoitwa `verify_availability` chenye vigezo viwili: agizo na hifadhidata ya bidhaa, kwa mpangilio huo.
1. `verify_availability` inapaswa:
   - toa `ValueError` yenye ujumbe `Product ID {product_id} is not in the inventory.` ikiwa bidhaa katika agizo haipo katika hifadhidata iliyotolewa.
   - toa `ValueError` yenye ujumbe `Insufficient stock for product ID {product_id}: requested {quantity}, available {available}.` ikiwa kiasi kilichotakiwa cha bidhaa ni kikubwa kuliko kiasi kilichopo katika hifadhidata iliyotolewa.
   - isitoe kosa ikiwa kila bidhaa katika agizo ipo kwa kiasi kilichotakiwa.
1. Unapaswa kuwa na kitendakazi kinachoitwa `get_discount_rate` chenye vigezo viwili: agizo na misimbo ya punguzo, kwa mpangilio huo.
1. `get_discount_rate` inapaswa:
   - rudisha `0.0` ikiwa `discount_code` ya agizo ni `None`.
   - toa `ValueError` yenye ujumbe `Invalid discount code: {discount_code}.` ikiwa `discount_code` ya agizo si mojawapo ya misimbo ya punguzo iliyotolewa.
   - rudisha kiwango cha punguzo cha `discount_code` ya agizo vinginevyo.
1. Unapaswa kuwa na kitendakazi kinachoitwa `process_order` chenye vigezo vinne: nambari ya agizo la kusindika, rekodi ya maagizo, hifadhidata ya bidhaa, na misimbo ya punguzo, kwa mpangilio huo.
1. `process_order` inapaswa kutumia `find_order`, `verify_availability`, na `get_discount_rate` kusindika agizo, na kushughulikia `ValueError` wanayotokeza ili kuitwa `process_order` isitoe kosa lolote.
1. Wakati hakuna agizo katika rekodi ya maagizo lililotolewa linalolingana na nambari ya agizo iliyotolewa, `process_order` inapaswa:
   - chapisha `Error processing order {order_id}: {error message}`, ambapo `{error message}` ni ujumbe wa `ValueError` uliotolewa na `find_order`, na isichapishe chochote kingine.
   - acha hifadhidata na misimbo ya punguzo iliyotolewa isibadilike.
1. Wakati agizo likipo lakini haliwezi kutimizwa, kwa sababu bidhaa haipo katika hifadhidata, kwa sababu kiasi kilichotakiwa hakipo, au kwa sababu msimbo wa punguzo si halali, `process_order` inapaswa:
   - chapisha `Error processing order {order_id}: {error message}`, ambapo `{error message}` ni ujumbe wa `ValueError` uliotolewa na `verify_availability` au `get_discount_rate`.
   - weka `status` ya agizo kuwa `failed`.
   - acha hifadhidata na misimbo ya punguzo iliyotolewa isibadilike.
   - chapisha `Order {order_id} processing completed. Current status: failed`.
1. Wakati agizo linaweza kutimizwa, `process_order` inapaswa:
   - chapisha `Order {order_id} total: ${total}`, kwa punguzo lililotumika na jumla ikizungushwa hadi sehemu mbili za desimali.
   - weka `status` ya agizo kuwa `processed`.
   - punguza kiasi cha bidhaa kilichotakiwa kutoka hifadhidata iliyotolewa.
   - ondoa msimbo wa punguzo ulio tumika na agizo kutoka misimbo ya punguzo iliyotolewa, ili kila msimbo utumike mara moja tu.
   - chapisha `Order {order_id} processing completed. Current status: processed`.

**Kumbuka:** Umepatiwa hifadhidata, rekodi ya maagizo, na misimbo ya punguzo ambayo unaweza kutumia kujaribu msimbo wako. Ita vitendakazi vyako kujaribu, ikiwa ni pamoja na matukio yanayotokeza makosa, lakini toa maoni kwenye wito hizo kabla ya kuendesha vipimo, au zinaweza kuathiri vipimo.

def find_order(order_id, order_history):
    for order in order_history:
        if order['order_id'] == order_id:
            return order
    raise ValueError(f'Order ID {order_id} not found in order history.')

def set_order_status(order, status):
    order['status'] = status

def get_discount_rate(order, discount_codes):
    discount_code = order['discount_code']
    if discount_code is None:
        return 0.0
    if discount_code not in discount_codes:
        raise ValueError(f'Invalid discount code: {discount_code}.')
    return discount_codes[discount_code]

def consume_discount_code(order, discount_codes):
    discount_code = order['discount_code']
    if discount_code is not None:
        del discount_codes[discount_code]

def verify_availability(order, inventory):
    products = order['products']
    for product in products:
        product_id = product['product_id']
        quantity = product['quantity']
        if product_id not in inventory:
            raise ValueError(f'Product ID {product_id} is not in the inventory.')
        available = inventory[product_id]['quantity']
        if available < quantity:
            raise ValueError(
                f'Insufficient stock for product ID {product_id}: '
                f'requested {quantity}, available {available}.'
            )

def calculate_total(order, inventory):
    products = order['products']
    total = 0.0
    for product in products:
        product_id = product['product_id']
        price = inventory[product_id]['price']
        quantity = product['quantity']
        total += price * quantity
    return total

def reserve_stock(order, inventory):
    products = order['products']
    for product in products:
        product_id = product['product_id']
        inventory[product_id]['quantity'] -= product['quantity']

def process_order(order_id, order_history, inventory, discount_codes):
    try:
        order = find_order(order_id, order_history)
    except ValueError as e:
        print(f'Error processing order {order_id}: {e}')
        return

    try:
        verify_availability(order, inventory)
        discount_rate = get_discount_rate(order, discount_codes)
        total = calculate_total(order, inventory)
    except ValueError as e:
        set_order_status(order, 'failed')
        print(f'Error processing order {order_id}: {e}')
    else:
        reserve_stock(order, inventory)
        consume_discount_code(order, discount_codes)
        set_order_status(order, 'processed')
        print(f'Order {order_id} total: ${total * (1 - discount_rate):.2f}')

    print(f'Order {order_id} processing completed. Current status: {order["status"]}')
