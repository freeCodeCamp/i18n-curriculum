Ifuatayo, utahesabu umbali wa kila joto kutoka wastani kwa kutumia list comprehension. Hapa kuna ukumbusho wa sintaksia:

```py
numbers = [1, 2, 3]
doubled = [num * 2 for num in numbers]
print(doubled) # [2, 4, 6]
```

Kisha, chapisha `differences` badala ya `temperatures`.
